# Interview Questions

## Q1. What is TCP/IP?

TCP/IP is the protocol suite used for communication over the Internet.

## Q2. How many layers are in the TCP/IP Model?

4 Layers.

## Q3. What is the difference between TCP and UDP?

TCP is connection-oriented and reliable.

UDP is connectionless and faster.

## Q4. Which protocol is used to test connectivity?

Ping (ICMP).
