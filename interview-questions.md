# Interview Questions

## Q1. What is a Network?

A network is a group of connected devices that communicate with each other.

---

## Q2. What is LAN?

A Local Area Network connects devices within a small geographical area.

---

## Q3. What is WAN?

A Wide Area Network connects devices across large geographical areas.

---

## Q4. Difference between Router and Switch?

Router connects different networks.

Switch connects devices within the same network.
