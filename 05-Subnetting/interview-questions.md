# Interview Questions

## Q1. What is Subnetting?

Subnetting is the process of dividing one network into multiple smaller networks.

---

## Q2. Why is Subnetting used?

To reduce broadcast traffic and improve network management.

---

## Q3. What is Network ID?

The first IP address in a subnet.

---

## Q4. What is Broadcast Address?

The last IP address in a subnet.

---

## Q5. How many usable hosts are available in a /24 network?

254 Hosts
